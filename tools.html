Board Game Prompt Builder

For anyone who wants to play a board game against an AI chat

Pick a game and how you want to play, and get a ready-to-paste prompt that keeps the AI honest about the board.

  • Chess, checkers, Connect Four, tic-tac-toe, Reversi, Go 9×9, Battleship, Mancala, or any game you type
  • Who goes first, how hard it plays, and its manner, from quiet to pirate
  • Extras: teach me, hints, undo, and the rules explained first
  • Makes the AI repeat each move, redraw the board every turn and check its own move is legal
  • Two fix-it prompts for when the board drifts or a move looks illegal

Works with any AI chat. Nothing you pick is sent anywhere; you copy the prompt yourself.

Cold Night Check

For RVs and trailers lived in through an Alberta winter

Pick your Alberta town and see how cold the next day and week will get, and what that cold means for your water lines and propane.

  • The coldest hour in the next 24 hours, with wind chill, and the forecast lows for the week
  • Steps that light up as it gets colder: exposed lines at 0 °C, the hose at −15 °C, propane pressure from −18 °C down
  • Only published propane pressure figures, no made-up in-between values
  • Forecast from Environment and Climate Change Canada, fetched in your browser

57 Alberta towns. Your town is remembered in your browser only; nothing else is stored.
