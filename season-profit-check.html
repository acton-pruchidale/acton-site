Season Profit Check

One job can look fine and a whole season still come up short. Log each job as you go: what you charged, the person-hours, the kilometres and the materials. This page adds up the season, takes off the fixed costs a single job never shows, and tells you which jobs paid and which didn't.

Your season

Fixed costs are the ones you pay whether you work or not: insurance, the blower or plow's yearly upkeep, phone, ads, a trailer payment. The CA$ per km covers fuel, tires and wear; if you don't track your own, pick a figure you trust and keep it the same all season.
