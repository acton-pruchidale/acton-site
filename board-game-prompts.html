Good to know

  
    
    
  • Keep the real board in front of you (a physical set or a free board app) and trust it over the chat. Chat AIs can play a decent game, but they are not chess engines and they sometimes misremember a piece.
    • 
    
  • Short games work best. Tic-tac-toe, Connect Four and checkers stay accurate the longest; long chess games drift more after move 30.
    • 
    
  • If a game gets long, start a new chat and paste the starting prompt plus the current position. A fresh chat remembers the board better than a long one.
    • 
    
  • Hidden-information games like Battleship need trust: the AI says where its ships are at the end, so you can check it didn't move them.
